Who may enter
The Competition is open to full-time students (including undergraduates, master's and doctoral candidates) cur -
rently enrolled at overseas universities and research institutions, with 60% non-Chinese nationals, major, or year 
of study. For the purposes of these Rules, "overseas universities" refers to higher education institutions and 
research organizations established in countries other than China (including regions outside Mainland China, Hong 
Kong SAR, Macao SAR, and Chinese Taipei) that hold independent accreditation and are authorized to inde-
pendently confer academic qualifications or degrees.
Registration and submission share the same deadline: October 20, 2026, 23:59 (Beijing Time, UTC+8) ù i.e., 
15:59 UTC | 17:00 London (BST) | 09:00 US Pacific (PDT) | 01:00 Tokyo (Oct 21) | 03:00 Sydney (Oct 21). A bilingual 
email reminder will be sent 24 hours before the deadline.
Teaming
Teams of 2û5 students; cross-year, cross-major, cross-university teaming (including cross-border collaborations) 
is welcome, provided that all team members are from institutions outside Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, 
Macao SAR, and Chinese Taipei. Each participant may join only one team across both the Domestic and Interna-
tional Tracks; duplicate registration is invalid.
Language
English is the official language of the International Track; submissions, presentations, and reviews are all conduct-
ed in English.
Note: To ensure the international character of this track, at least 60% of a teamÆ s members should be 
non-Chinese nationals. Track assignment is determined by the organizer based on membersÆ nationali -
ties and institutions.
