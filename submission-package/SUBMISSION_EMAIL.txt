EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BUSINESS VALUE FOR SMEs:
======================================================================
Over 82% of small businesses fail not from lack of hard work, but from cash flow blindness. LEDGER ZERO transforms small business finance from passive, error-prone bookkeeping into an active, autonomous financial participant:

1. POS Quick Sell & Automatic 'Hisab': Cashier or owner scans/selects products on the register (/pos). In one click, checkout issues a paid invoice, automatically deducts physical stock inventory, and settles cash records—eliminating 100% of manual spreadsheet bookkeeping.
2. 14-Day Cash Buffer & Liquidity Gap Alert: Real-time cash forecasting calculates daily net balance (Gap = Cash in Hand - Payables Due in 7 Days). If a deficit is detected, the system immediately triggers a high-priority red alert and formulates an emergency recovery plan days before bills come due.
3. Intelligent Accounts Receivable (AR) Recovery: Categorizes unpaid customer bills into 4 aging tiers and drafts polite, professional collection notices with automated early settlement discounts (e.g., 2% off if paid in 48h).
4. SME Financing Option Matching: Analyzes verified cash flow trajectory to match appropriate credit instruments (Invoice Factoring vs. Working Capital Line), comparing effective APR vs. gross margins.
5. Human-in-the-Loop Safe Execution: AI proposes structured actions, but is strictly prohibited from mutating the ledger without verified human owner approval.
